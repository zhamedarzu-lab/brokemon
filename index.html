BROKEMON

The only thing you're catching is a break.

You wake up in the park on Route 1 with three dollars, a debt, and a body that needs feeding every few hours. The Mart will not serve you looking like that. The Heights will not let you past the gate. Everything else is up to how many days you can string together.
